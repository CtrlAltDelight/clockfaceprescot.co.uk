Welcome to The Clock Face, Prescot

The Clock Face is a welcoming pub, perfectly positioned in Prescot with home cooked food and quality cask ales at its heart. Following our fantastic refurbishment, the building is back to its former glory with a new modern look, big relaxing spaces and snug fireside corners to enjoy. Warm, inviting and family friendly we’ve a great atmosphere with exceptional service, so come and join us.

We are proud to be a pub everyone can enjoy and where everyone is welcome.

The Clock Face, Prescot is a historical and magnificent Georgian building, with ties to Lord Derby as the former dowry house and is in close proximity to Knowsley Hall. The pubs positioning in the historic watchmaking town gives it a great location in Prescot.

We’re a destination for dining and drinking, with great spaces for any celebration or occasion.

View Our Menus

Enjoy well-loved pub classics, familiar and tasty, using only the freshest, locally sourced seasonal produce. Don’t forget to accompany your food with a pint of excellent quality award winning Thwaites Ale or choose from our great range of spirits and wines for something different. Whether it’s a light bite, a three-course dinner or you’re just popping in for a well-earned pint, our varied menus mean there’s something for everyone.

Take a seat in our stylish, modern and relaxed dining areas for a great meal out.
